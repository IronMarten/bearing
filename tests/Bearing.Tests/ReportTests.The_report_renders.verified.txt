-- PROJECT STABILITY vs ABSTRACTNESS ---------------------------
   I = Ce/(Ce+Ca), low = much depends on it. A = share of types that are
   abstract or interfaces.
   The MAIN SEQUENCE is the balance where how abstract a project is
   matches how much depends on it: a project much depended on earns
   that by being abstract, and one that depends on much can afford to
   be concrete. D = |A + I - 1| is how far off that balance a project
   sits.
